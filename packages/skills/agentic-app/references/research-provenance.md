# Research and Source Provenance

External facts that materially influence product, compliance, architecture, pricing, compatibility, or domain behavior should be traceable.

For durable research, capture:
- source title and URL/reference
- checked date
- claim supported
- confidence/authority
- freshness or expiry if time-sensitive
- whether the source is normative, advisory, or anecdotal

Do not copy large external documents into repository memory. Distill the relevant claim and link the source. Re-check stale time-sensitive claims before using them for consequential changes.
