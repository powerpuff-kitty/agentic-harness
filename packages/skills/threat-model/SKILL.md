# Threat Model

## Goal
Produce an evidence-backed threat model for a proposed change or existing system.

## Workflow
1. Identify assets, trust boundaries, actors, entry points, dependencies, and data flows.
2. Enumerate plausible threats including spoofing, tampering, repudiation, disclosure, denial of service, privilege escalation, prompt injection, confused deputy behavior, and supply-chain risks.
3. Rank by impact and realistic exploitability.
4. Map mitigations to concrete controls and verification.
5. Record accepted risks and unresolved assumptions.

Do not claim a vulnerability exists without evidence; distinguish threat hypotheses from confirmed findings.
