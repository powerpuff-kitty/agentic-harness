# Product

## Problem

Describe the user or business problem in observable terms.

## Users

List primary users, their goals, and important constraints.

## Scope

Define current capabilities and boundaries.

## Non-goals

State what the project intentionally does not solve.

## Success criteria

Define measurable outcomes and acceptance signals.

## Open questions

Keep unresolved product decisions explicit. Move accepted consequential choices into an ADR and reflect the resulting current state here.
