# Pricing

Pricing is not finalized.

## Current principle

Keep the core harness useful for individual developers and open-source adoption. Any future paid offering should monetize capabilities that create operational value beyond the local CLI, such as managed distribution, organization-wide governance, hosted policy/eval management, analytics, compliance workflows, or enterprise support.

## Questions to validate

- Is the primary buyer an individual developer, engineering manager, platform team, or agency?
- Is value better measured per developer, repository, organization, policy pack, or managed workflow?
- Which capabilities create recurring hosted/operational cost?
- Which features must remain local/offline for trust and adoption?

Do not publish tier names, prices, limits, or willingness-to-pay claims until they are accepted decisions backed by evidence.
