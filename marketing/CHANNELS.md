# Channels

Potential channels should be evaluated by audience fit and feedback quality, not vanity reach.

- GitHub: primary discovery, source, releases, examples, issues, and contributor loop.
- Technical articles: explain architecture, migration from monolithic agent files, scoped instructions, and deterministic governance.
- Developer communities: validate terminology, onboarding friction, and interoperability needs.
- Demo video/short clips: show `ah init`, guided setup, multi-agent use, and audit/gate flows.
- Launch communities: useful once installation and first-run experience are polished.

Track channel, audience, hypothesis, asset, result, and learning for experiments.
